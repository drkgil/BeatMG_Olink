# BeatMG_Olink
### Study design and samples
Serum samples from the BeatMG study (NCT02110706) were used from which all patients had provided informed consent to participate in research sample collection. The clinical data and biosamples were made available to the study team by the BeatMG principal investigators. Analysis of clinical metrics related to proteomic analysis at study entrance has been previously published. 

This study used de-identified proteomic data generated from participants enrolled in the BeatMG trial, a randomized controlled study comparing rituximab versus placebo. Only participants with serum samples at baseline, week 24, and week 52 were used for longitudinal modeling.


### Serum Proteomics 
Proteomic analysis was performed by the Olink platform (Olink Bioscience, Uppsala, Sweden), which uses Proximity Extension Assay (PEA) as previously described. Protein abundance was quantified using Olink proximity extension assay panels (Inflammation and Inflammation II panels with 730 inflammatory markers). Protein levels were represented as normalized protein eXpression (NPX) values, Olink’s log2-scaled relative quantification metric.  Only proteins that passed the quality control of the Olink platform were used.
